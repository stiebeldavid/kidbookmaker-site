Questions

Do I need a finished story?

No. A single sentence or a rough idea works. A full manuscript works too. We shape it into pages either way and keep your words and ideas at the heart of it.

Can I tell the story out loud instead of typing it?

Yes. Record a voice note right in the form, or upload an audio file.

Who illustrates it?

We do. Every page is painted for your book in one consistent storybook style. You don't draw anything, and you can ask for changes to any picture.

How long does it take?

Your part takes a few minutes. Your first preview usually arrives the same day. Printing and shipping come after you approve.

Can I print it?

Yes. Every book is made to be printed. Once you approve the preview, we print a real hardcover.

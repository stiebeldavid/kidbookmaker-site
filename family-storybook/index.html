Questions

How many people can be in the book?

As many as the story needs. Photos help us draw people to look like themselves; you can add up to 3, and a group photo is a great way to include everyone.

Can our pet be in it?

Of course. Pets make wonderful characters. Add a photo and mention them in the story.

Does it have to be a true story?

No. It can be your family's real story or a made-up adventure starring all of you.

How long does it take?

Your part takes a few minutes. Your first preview usually arrives the same day. Printing and shipping come after you approve.

Can I make it in Spanish?

Yes. Tell us the story in Spanish and the whole book, and our emails, will be in Spanish.

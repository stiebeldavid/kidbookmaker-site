Questions

Who makes the book?

Your child writes the story. We turn it into pages, illustrate every scene, and lay it out as a real picture book, keeping their words and ideas at the heart of it.

How long does it take?

Your first preview usually arrives within a few days. Printing and shipping come after you approve.

What does it cost?

Books are priced per copy, and you'll see the price with your preview. There's nothing to pay until you approve the book.

Can I change things?

Yes. Ask for as many tweaks as you want before printing: words, pictures, colors, even what the hero looks like. Just reply to our email.

Questions

Do I need the person's photo?

No, but it helps. With a clear photo we draw the character to look like them. Without one, describe them and we'll take it from there.

Can I add a dedication?

Yes. Include it with your story, or reply to our email with it, and we'll put it in the book.

How long does it take?

Your part takes a few minutes. Your first preview usually arrives the same day. Printing and shipping come after you approve, so leave time before the occasion.

Can I see it before I pay?

Yes. You see the full preview first, and there's nothing to pay until you approve the book.

Can I print it?

Yes. Every book is made to be printed. Once you approve the preview, we print a real hardcover.

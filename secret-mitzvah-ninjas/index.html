Who did all these mitzvahs?

Something mysterious is happening in Maple Hollow. Old Mrs. Feldman’s weedy garden is suddenly perfect. The Levys’ mountain of dirty pots is sparkling clean. Morah Rivky’s messy playroom tidies itself up while she steps out for snack. And every time, the whole town cries out the same thing:

“It must have been the Secret Mitzvah Ninjas!”

Over fences and out of pantries they come, quick and quiet, doing one kindness after another. They don’t want a thank-you. They don’t even want anyone to know their names.

A joyful, rhyming read-aloud with shout-along lines your kids will be yelling by the second page, and a sweet lesson tucked inside: a mitzvah can be quiet, a mitzvah can be small, and anybody can be a Secret Mitzvah Ninja. Even YOU!
